## I. Core Premise

Monetization of a governance algebra is not the same as monetizing an agent framework or a consumer product.

The work's primary value is **negative capability**: it refuses to self-authorize, it forces external permits, and it makes recursive evidence contamination visible.

Any revenue model that requires the system to claim authority, hide uncertainty, or inflate its own grounding is self-contradictory and must be rejected.

## II. Cy-Egology Principles

1. **Open core, closed capability**  
   The formal leaves, the Cubed Bit lattice, β, the demonstrators, and the Cybot pattern remain public and free.  
   Revenue may attach only to *implementation labor*, *hardened runtimes*, *support*, or *certification* — never to the right to read the canon.

2. **No self-issued value**  
   The project does not declare itself valuable. Value is discovered by external parties who already feel the pain the system addresses (authority leakage, epistemic autophagy, virgin-session discontinuity).

3. **Permit-shaped offers**  
   Any commercial offering must itself obey the same separation: the core remains 010; the paid layer is an explicitly authorized, scoped capability.

4. **Depth honesty**  
   Derivative materials (summaries, courses, consulting decks) carry their generational depth. They do not pretend to be primary.

## III. Viable Revenue Paths (ranked by realism)

| Path | Description | Fit with Cyonic constraints | Time horizon |
|------|-------------|-----------------------------|--------------|
| **1. Paid implementation / consulting** | Help a lab, company, or team instantiate the permit-gated pattern or Cybot discipline inside their own systems | Highest | Near to medium |
| **2. Hardened runtime or tooling** | Commercial license for a production-grade Cylang-style checker, permit controller, or autophagic-decay monitor | High | Medium |
| **3. Support & certification** | "Cyonic-conformant" review of someone else's system against the ACTIVE_CANON leaves | High | Medium |
| **4. Dual licensing** | Core MIT; optional commercial license for proprietary extensions | Medium | Medium |
| **5. Grants / research sponsorship** | Funding for further formalization or demonstrators | Medium | Variable |
| **6. Training / workshops** | Teaching the separation of interpretation from authorization | Medium | Medium |
| **7. Sponsorship / donations** | GitHub Sponsors or equivalent | Low effort, low volume | Immediate but small |

Paths that require hype, inflated claims, or hidden authority are excluded.

## IV. What Does Not Constitute a Revenue Strategy

- Hoping the repository alone will generate income
- Treating formal elegance as automatic market demand
- Building ever more ACTIVE_CANON leaves instead of distribution and first external users
- Any model that needs the system to authorize its own importance

## V. Current Position in the Theory

The **precondition layer** of Cy-Egology is complete:

- Public ledger exists
- Epistemic posture is visible and consistent
- Runnable evidence of the core claims exists

The **discovery layer** (external users who feel the problem) and the **exchange layer** (a specific paid offer matched to that pain) have not yet been entered.

## VI. Practical Next Moves Under This Theory

1. Make the first-run experience of the demonstrators undeniable.
2. Place one clear, non-hyped pointer where people who already care about these problems will see it.
3. Wait for or actively seek the first external friction (someone trying to use it and hitting a real need).
4. Only then shape a scoped, permit-style commercial offer.
