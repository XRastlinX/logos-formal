## 1. Matrix Vertex Identification

The Cubed Bit geometry defines the state of a payload based on its (Authority, Interpretation, Effect) coordinates:

*   **`000` (The Raw Witness):** Exact byte capture. No interpretation, no authority, no physical effect.
*   **`010` (The Pure Interpreter):** A parser or model evaluating the semantics of `000`. Does not claim authority or apply effects. (This is the strict domain of the `logos-formal` AI integration).
*   **`101` (The Sovereign Actuator):** Possesses authority (`1`) and triggers physical effect (`1`), but does not invent interpretations (`0`). It strictly executes the proven interpretation provided by `010` under the authority of `100`.

## 2. The Illegal Vertices

Several vertices represent catastrophic conflations of the governance boundaries:

*   **`110` (The Authoritative Interpreter):** An interpreter that claims the right to authorize its own semantic leaps. This bypasses the Sovereign Apply constraint.
*   **`111` (The Autonomous Singularity):** A system that interprets, authorizes, and executes independently. This is the exact collapse that `logos-formal` explicitly prevents. 

All payloads routed via the CyExchange Corridor must clearly demarcate their coordinate. The `required_state` field strictly enforces that an Envelope does not illegally cross from `010` into `100` or `101`.
