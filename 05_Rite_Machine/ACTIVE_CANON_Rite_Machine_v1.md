## II. The Geometric Governance States

### The Child State ($010$)
A child explores, interprets, imagines, and simulates. They possess a rich interior ($\nu$) but possess no legal mandate to authorize ($A=0$) and no physical capacity to actuate ($E=0$). This is the $010$ boundary condition: **Interpretation only. No authority. No effect.**

### The Infant State (Null-Prime, gap=1)
The pure beginning: no interior, no residual, no frame. Pure identity and zero-weight potential. Mapped to **qemic / $cy_\emptyset$**.

### The Adult State ($101$)
An adult carries responsibility (residual = 1), possesses an established identity (frame = $\phi(i,r)$), and holds agency (capability = 101).

### The "Little Death" (Collapse Operator)
Systems must be able to heal, reset, or re-temper. The Collapse Operator strips the residual, frame, and geometry, returning the cord to the $000$ pure witness state, explicitly forcing a restart of the Seven-Stage machine.
